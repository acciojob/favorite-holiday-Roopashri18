Goa, India


Goa is a small coastal state on India's west coast, famous for its golden beaches, Portuguese-era churches, lively markets and relaxed atmosphere. It is a perfect place to unwind with friends or family.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Baga Beach for water sports and nightlife
  2. Basilica of Bom Jesus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
  3. Fort Aguada with its view over the sea
  4. Dudhsagar Falls for a scenic nature trip
  5. Anjuna Flea Market for shopping and local crafts

Must-Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry with rice
  • Bebinca, a layered dessert
  • Pork Vindaloo, a spicy classic
  • Prawn Balchão, a tangy pickle-style dish
